# Cybersecurity Foundations and SOC Readiness

A SOC junior does not 'hack back.' They count, threshold, and write one honest sentence. AUTH_FAIL lines that pile onto one user are a burst. Bursts are not proof of an attacker — they are proof you should look.

The lab counts AUTH_FAIL tokens and flags users at threshold 3. You will also practice not pasting passwords into the incident note. If the note needs a secret, the note is wrong.

Privacy: these logs are synthetic. Never import real school logs into this pack.
