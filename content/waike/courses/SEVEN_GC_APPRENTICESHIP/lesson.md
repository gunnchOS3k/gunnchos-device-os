# 7GC AI-RAN Research Apprenticeship

Shannon capacity C = B log2(1+SNR) is an upper bound under AWGN, not a 7GC field measurement. A 1 MHz slice at SNR=3 linear yields about 2 Mbit/s. Real RAN overhead, scheduling, and hardware make that number optimistic.

Apprentices list assumptions before they quote C. This seed forces the list: AWGN, single user, no overhead, not a field measurement. If a poster drops the list, the poster is overclaiming.
