# IT Support and Hardware Foundations

A ticket is a promise to restore someone's work, not a chat thread. Hardware foundations start at power, then storage, then memory, then the OS. Swapping parts before naming the subsystem is how labs waste DIMMs.

Symptoms map poorly to parts if you skip questions: did it slow at boot, crash one app, or scream from the fan? Low RAM plus app crash points at memory. Slow boot plus almost-full disk points at storage. High CPU plus fan noise points at cooling — still not a new motherboard.

Collect logs before you unscrew anything. This seed uses a virtual ticket; no live chassis. Kinesthetic add-on (classroom): point at the RAM slots on a donor board while the lab classifies the ticket.
