# Hardware Engineering

A voltage divider is two resistors in series. Vout = Vin * R2/(R1+R2) when you take the tap across R2. 5 V, 1 kΩ, 3 kΩ → 3.75 V. Current is Vin/(R1+R2).

Owner program is a 54-byte stub. This seed is Ohm's-law arithmetic plus a warning: do not build it on a live 120 V circuit. Paper and the lab are enough for digital acceptance of the *seed*, not of a hardware engineering degree.
