# Cloud and DevOps

Clusters forgive nothing you forgot to reject at the gate. A class manifest needs an image with a tag, a positive replica count, a legal port, and no privileged flag. Privileged containers are forbidden in this classroom cluster even if a blog said they are 'easier for hardware.'

Owner program file for this course is a four-line stub. The product seed is the validator. You still do not have a full DevOps course — you have one gate.
