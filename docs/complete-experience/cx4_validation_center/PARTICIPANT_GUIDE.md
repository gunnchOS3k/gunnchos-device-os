# Participant Guide

## What this session is

You are helping us understand how gunnchOS feels for real people. This is a guided checklist with ratings — not a test you can fail as a person.

## What you will do

- Enter a session code from the person hosting the session
- Optionally adjust accessibility settings (text size, contrast, simplified instructions, reduced motion)
- Agree to a short privacy / consent summary
- Complete one task at a time
- Rate how it went
- Optionally add notes, photos, or files if you agreed to that

## How long it may take

Your host will show an expected duration. Many sessions are about 15–60 minutes depending on the tasks.

## How to ask for help

Use **I need help**, or tell the host. You can pause anytime.

## Ratings

You will see labeled scales (for example, ease from “Very difficult” to “Very easy”). Free-text boxes are optional. You may choose **Prefer not to answer** for optional scales.

## Evidence

If asked, you can add a screenshot, photo, video, audio, file, or note. If your browser cannot capture something, you can upload a file instead.

## Privacy

Responses stay on the local Validation Center unless an operator exports them for the study. Private items are kept out of public share exports.

## Stopping at any time

Use **Pause** or **Stop session**. You do not have to finish everything. Your progress is saved so you can resume if you want.
