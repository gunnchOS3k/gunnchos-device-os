# VXP Design Constitution

1. **Honesty first** — never invent recent activity, catalogs, backups, or provider health.
2. **Brand, not host** — product identity is gunnchOS / Living Workspace; `ANDROID_CAPSULE` is host metadata, not hero copy.
3. **Purpose over chrome** — Spaces explain why they exist before listing features.
4. **Geometric icons** — no emoji as product icons.
5. **Local type** — no network fonts required for the shell.
6. **Motion is optional** — Assist reduce-motion and `prefers-reduced-motion` win.
7. **Touch ≥ 44px** — dock, rail, and primary actions meet minimum targets.
8. **Preserve contracts** — surface IDs, `data-action` attributes, provider URLs, offline honesty.
9. **No trade-dress cloning** — not iOS/Android/Windows skins; not purple SaaS glass.
10. **Evidence classes** — browser/fixture ≠ physical Pixel; never convert PENDING to PASS without authentic capture.
