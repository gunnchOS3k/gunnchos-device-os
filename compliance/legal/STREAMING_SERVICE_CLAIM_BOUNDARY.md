# Streaming Service Claim Boundary

**Do not claim Netflix, Hulu, Disney+, Max, Prime Video, or other DRM service certification.**

## Allowed

- Open service URL in external browser tab
- Document compatibility/readiness in `streaming_certification/` (PR #45)
- Display honest disclaimers in Media Hub

## Not allowed

- "Works with Netflix" without service certification evidence
- Widevine/PlayReady integration claims without CDM license and test evidence
- HDCP external display certification without hardware tests
